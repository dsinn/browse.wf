    Riven Calculator

    Every trait (positive and negative) on your Riven has a random multiplier between 0.9x and 1.1x (or -10% to +10%). This tool shows the range each trait can roll, and grades the values on a Riven you own.

    Riven Dispositions were retired in Hotfix 44.1.3: every weapon now uses the same 1.2x multiplier, so only the kind of weapon matters.

    Spliced traits

    A Riven can hold one spliced trait, which is always positive. Per the Warframe Wiki, a spliced trait keeps the higher roll of the two traits it was made from, so a high grade is likely.
